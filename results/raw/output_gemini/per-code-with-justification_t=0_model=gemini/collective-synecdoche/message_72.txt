User: To the Editor: Concerning "Once a Hub of Strife, Boston Woos Black Tourists"(Dec. 8), an African-American heritage trail can be found closer to New York. Among the sites are the W. E. B. Du Bois River Garden in Great Barrington, Mass., Du Bois's hometown, and the Col. John Ashley House in Sheffield, Mass., where in the early 1780s Elizabeth Freeman, also known as Mumbet, began the fight that helped end slavery in Massachusetts. For more information, see www.africanamericantrail.org.
